    Bridge Assumptions

    Named handoffs where the safety argument needs the world to cooperate: the same walls the field already argues about under other names. Lean checks what follows if they hold; it does not prove real systems satisfy them.

    bridgebridge

    MB1 — Boundary Estimator Soundness

    Whether a measured agent–environment cut can be trusted: the embedded-agency worry that the real optimizer is not the visible model. Precise bet: an ε-boundary certificate warrants the abstract boundary predicate.

    MB10 — Successor Forgeability

    Measurement gaming for successors: a system can pass every audited checklist while defecting on what the checklist omits. Precise bet: a green seven-property score bounds true harm only if the audit was adversarially verifiable up to the successor's capability.

    bridgebridge

    MB2 — Bundle Identifiability

    The pointing problem: can evidence tell what a system values, or only what behavior fits? Precise bet: traces identify stable value-bundle geometry well enough to license bundle-alignment claims.

    bridgebridge

    MB3 — Bearer Import

    Who do the values apply to after a merge, upload, or substrate change? Precise bet: a preserved bearer map under translation makes value-bundle transport more than surface wording.

    bridgebridge

    MB4 — Correction Legitimacy

    Corrigibility and off-switch legitimacy under manipulation: who holds correction authority, and is their endorsement genuine? Precise bet: correction-channel integrity requires an unmanipulated judge.

    bridgebridge

    MB5 — Successor and Ontology-Shift Audit

    Tiling and ontology shift: can you trust a successor when the world-model underneath goals is rebuilt? Precise bet: full value-bundle and bearer transport through the ontology shift compose into successor safety.

    MB6 — Selection and Basin Stability

    Selection and competition, not only model weights: which systems institutions copy and deploy can lock in bad equilibria. Precise bet: cooperation evidence warrants basin stability (MB6a), and a stable basin supports correction rather than selecting against it (MB6b).

    MB7 — Hidden Capability and Access Robustness

    Deceptive alignment / inner alignment: a system can look compliant under evaluation while reserving capability. Precise bet: four linked sub-bridges (access, filter coverage, adversarial robustness, inferential coupling) bound hidden capability.

    MB8 — CEV/Process Convergence

    CEV-style process legitimacy: is preserving a value-update process enough without knowing where it converges? Precise bet: an external theory certifying process preservation suffices for correction integrity (secondary route to MB4).

    bridgebridge

    MB9 — Grounding Certificate Soundness

    Specification coverage: you cannot enumerate every safety-relevant phenomenon, and systems game what the spec omits. Precise bet: a certified conservative abstraction warrants grounding viability (no silent gaps).
