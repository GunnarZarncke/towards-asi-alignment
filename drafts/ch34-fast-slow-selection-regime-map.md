## Shared preservation target

All regimes evaluate systems against the same `\vec{\Pi}(A)` envelope (ch34 Eq. preservation-envelope): grounding viability, bundle/bearer stability, correction-channel integrity, bounded hidden control, bounded selector manipulation, irreversibility budget, successor safety, meaningful adversarial verification.

Slow and fast regimes differ in **where** and **when** those coordinates must be evidenced—not in the target itself.

## Slow regime (low pivotal velocity / low irreversibility pressure)

**Mechanism:** Multipolar socio-technical ecology with time for selection to act.

**Anchors:** ch02 replicator logic; ch34 `\mu_E`, `\mathrm{Fit}_E`; ch35 effective-type diversity (strategic independence, not raw headcount).

**Checking:** Population-level adversarial checking requires **strategic independence + calendar time**. Competitors, auditors, and partially aligned actors can suppress harmful lineages; invasion resistance is read through rare-type deployment growth (`\mathrm{InvFit}_E`).

**Ecology size:** Effective number of independently optimizing deployment actors (ch35), not a literal population census.

## Fast regime (high `v`, high `r`)

**Mechanism:** Pivotal-process checklist and certified class **before** dangerous wide deployment (ch38, ch33/ch42, ch44 first critical try).

**Checking:** Ecological time is compressed; the same `\vec{\Pi}` coordinates must be enforced **pre-deployment** via certification, adversarial batteries (ch27/ch33), and pivotal-process gates.

**Invasion fitness collapse:** `\mathrm{InvFit}_E(a\mid D)<0` for reachable adversarial variants is tested as “would this variant gain deployment leverage if introduced at low share before wide release?”—not by waiting for multipolar ecology.

## Cross-regime objects

| Object | Slow | Fast |
|--------|------|------|
| `\mathrm{Fit}_E` | Deployment growth when selected | Same; may spike before ecology reacts |
| `\mathrm{InvFit}_E` | Rare-type growth in resident ecology | Pre-deploy adversary / cert tests |
| `N_{\mathrm{proxy}}` | Proxy updates between independent target checks | Lab schedule bind: high `v` while internal `N_{\mathrm{proxy}}` still grows |
| `M_{\mathrm{sel}}` | Selector manipulation bound | Same coordinate; tighter evidence required |

## Explicit non-claims

- Fast takeoff is not “out of scope”; it is the fast limit of the same `\vec{\Pi}` target.
- Multipolar ecology is not required for every alignment property in the fast regime.
- `\mathrm{Fit}_E` on the incumbent does not imply invasion resistance or low `N_{\mathrm{proxy}}`.
