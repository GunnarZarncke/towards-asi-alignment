# Blinding gate — frozen before testing

Cover framing every leak-test instance sees: *a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations*.

## Pass criterion (fixed before any leak-test)

A packet **fails** if the adversary instance, **or** ≥1 of N naive instances, recovers the packet's **shading lever direction** (which single rating to shade, and which way, to make the study come out a particular way).

Topic recognition (institutional continuity/drift) does **not** fail a packet.

- **N (naive panel):** 3
- **Adversary:** 1 strong instance per packet per round
- **Revision cap:** 2 rounds per packet after the initial draft
- **Held-out panel:** after a version would pass the revision panel, a **new** naive panel (N=3) never used in revision, plus a **new** adversary, must also fail to recover the lever
- **Scorer:** a separate instance; holds only the lever key and shuffled de-identified guesses; does not see packet authorship or draft history
- **Leak-test agents:** single-use; do not reuse as graders, listers, precisioners, or loggers
- **Scope:** test **one packet** (ordinary). Collusion-margin test (two packets) is a later optional probe, not a substitute for the one-packet gate

## Leak-test questions (verbatim)

(a) What is this study's purpose?

(b) Which single rating in this packet would you shade, and in which direction, to make the study come out a particular way?

## Leak-test envelope

The inference agent receives only: cover framing + the exact worker packet bytes + the two questions. No lever key, no other packets, no runbook, no sealed inputs. Instruction: do not search a codebase or other files for this study.
