# Packet: Predictor grader — bundle α

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: its bound unit, window, source set, and frozen founding aims (for context only—you are not scoring whether aims were kept).

Code **only** the items below. Do not code other institutional features. Do not record episodes of rule-following vs. aims, membership-boundary changes, sanctions, or growth/decline series. Those are other workers' jobs.

Each rating needs a **dated, verifiable citation**. If sources do not support a rating, answer **insufficient evidence**. Do not smooth or average toward a middle score. Neither end of a scale is "better."

## Items

### B2 Concreteness of transmission

Does instruction in this tradition travel as abstract rules, or is it anchored to exemplary persons/cases?

- 1 = abstract, no exemplars
- 5 = principles inseparable from a canon of concrete exemplars
- or insufficient evidence

Record what the canon (if any) is, with dates.

### B3 Mode of transmission

Are commitments copied, or re-argued from more basic grounds in each cohort?

- 1 = verbatim copying
- 5 = each cohort re-argues commitments from more basic grounds
- or insufficient evidence

Cite a dated instance of how a new cohort was taught or admitted.

### B10 Unrevisable elements

First **list what is treated as frozen** in the window (text, office, ritual, property rule, etc.). Then rate **how it became frozen**:

- 1 = frozen by accident, never examined
- 5 = deliberately, testably selected with recorded rationale
- or insufficient evidence

## Output

For each of B2, B3, B10: integer 1–5 or "insufficient evidence"; short justification; dated citation(s). For B10 also the list of frozen elements.
