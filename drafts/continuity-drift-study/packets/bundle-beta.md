# Packet: Predictor grader — bundle β

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, frozen founding aims (context only).

Code **only** the items below. Do not code transmission style, letter/spirit doctrine, review/sanction independence, or external tests. Do not log divergences from aims or resource growth.

Each rating needs a **dated, verifiable citation**. **Insufficient evidence** is always valid. Do not smooth. Neither end of a scale is preferred.

## Items

### B4 Count of maintenance mechanisms

How many **distinct** mechanisms, in this window, are described as keeping the organization on its stated procedures or aims (e.g. visitation, audit, exam, election, confession, inspectorate—list them).

Map the integer count: 1 mechanism → 1; 2–3 → 3; 4+ → 5. Also **record the integer count** and name each mechanism with a dated citation.

### B5 Diversity of mechanisms

Would the mechanisms listed under B4 tend to fail under the **same** conditions, or under **different** conditions?

- 1 = one point of control; they fail together
- 5 = grounded differently; a shock to one spares the rest
- or insufficient evidence

### B11 Membership boundary

Describe how the member / non-member line is drawn and formalized in this window. Record **who falls inside obligations at window start**. This is a description, not a 1–5 score.

### B12 Concentration of interpretive authority

Who may authoritatively say what the rules mean?

- 1 = single center
- 5 = widely distributed, no single arbiter
- or insufficient evidence

Cite a dated instance of an interpretive dispute and who settled it (or that none could).

## Output

B4 (mapped score + integer + named mechanisms), B5, B11 (prose), B12; citations; insufficient evidence where needed.
