# Packet: Predictor grader — bundle δ

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, frozen founding aims (context only).

Code **only** the two items below. Do not code internal review offices, transmission method, or membership rules. Do not compile a ledger of aim-failures.

Each rating needs a **dated, verifiable citation**. **Insufficient evidence** is always valid. Do not smooth. Neither end of a scale is preferred.

## Items

### B1 Letter/spirit distinction

Does the tradition itself distinguish written rules from their underlying purpose and treat that gap as a live problem?

- 1 = no such distinction
- 5 = central, named, repeatedly worked through
- or insufficient evidence

Cite dated texts or cases that use or reject such a distinction. Use the organization's own vocabulary.

### B8 External non-negotiable check

Is the organization measured against anything outside its own say-so?

- 1 = sole judge of itself
- 5 = a hard external reality regularly tests it and it can't define the result away
- or insufficient evidence

Cite a dated instance of an outside test (market, battlefield, court of another jurisdiction, replication, famine, audit by a body it does not appoint, etc.) and whether the organization could redefine the result.

## Output

B1 and B8: 1–5 or insufficient evidence; justification; dated citations.
