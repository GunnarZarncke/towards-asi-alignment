# Packet: Fact log — what is treated as unchangeable

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

One case: bound unit, window, source set.

List items treated as unchangeable in this window (text, office, ritual, property rule, etc.). For each, cite **how sources say it became so** (decree with reasons, custom with no discussion, accident of survival, etc.). Quote the rationale if one is recorded.

Do not rate accident vs design. Do not assign 1–5.

## Output

Item list with dated citations. None found after search is valid.
