# Packet: Fact log — what would stop each listed practice

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive one case bound and a **list of named maintenance practices** (no extra commentary).

For each practice, cite what historical shocks actually interrupted it, or what the organization’s own rules say would suspend it (war, vacancy of one office, loss of a fund, papal interdict, etc.).

Do not rate diversity. Do not assign 1–5. Do not discuss capture.

## Output

One row per input practice: practice; interrupting condition; citation; or insufficient evidence.
