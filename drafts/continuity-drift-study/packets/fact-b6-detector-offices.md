# Packet: Fact log — complaint and inspection offices (staffing)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

One case: bound unit, window, source set.

List every office or body that, in this window, is described as hearing complaints, inspecting, auditing, or judging members or decisions.

For each, record with dated citations:

- official name
- who appoints
- who pays or funds
- tenure / term rule
- who may dismiss or overrule
- one dated instance of appointment or removal, if any

Do not assign a number 1–5.

## Output

One block per office. If none found after search, write that.
