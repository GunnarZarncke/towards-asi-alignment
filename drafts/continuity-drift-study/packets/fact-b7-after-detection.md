# Packet: Fact log — what followed a detection

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

One case: bound unit, window, source set.

List dated events in which some party **claimed to have detected an error** (report, verdict, visitation finding, audit qualification, etc.).

For each event record: date; who detected; what they claimed; whether a reversal, removal, fine, or other status change followed within a cited period; citation.

Valid follow-up values: status change cited; statement only cited; insufficient evidence.

Do not rate “teeth.” Do not judge independence of the detector. Do not score the organization.

## Output

Dated event rows. None found after search is valid.
