# Packet: Fact log — outside tests

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

One case: bound unit, window, source set.

List dated occasions when something **outside the organization’s own published judgment** produced a result the organization had to live with (price, harvest, battle, foreign court, independent replication, unappointed inspector, etc.).

For each: date; what the outside process was; who controlled it; what the result was; whether sources show the organization redefining that result; citation.

Do not rate how “hard” the test was. Do not assign 1–5. Do not discuss drift.

## Output

Dated event rows. None found after search is valid.
