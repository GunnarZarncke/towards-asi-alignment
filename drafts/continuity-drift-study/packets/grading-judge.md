# Packet: Grading judge (craft, not conclusions)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one completed worker packet** (one grader's or logger's output) plus that worker's instructions and the case bound.

Check **craft only**:

1. Every non-descriptive rating has a dated, verifiable citation, or is marked insufficient evidence.
2. Every "insufficient evidence" looks like a genuine gap (not a way to skip an easy cite).
3. If you are shown two independent codes of the **same** bundle, report whether they agree within one point on each numeric item. If a gap is larger than one point, do **not** average them and do **not** host a discussion; record that a third coder is required from the evidence.

You are not told what the study is trying to show. Do not "fix" ratings to be more plausible. Do not infer a theory.

## Output

Pass / fail per check; list of citation problems; for double-codes, per-item agreement and whether a third coder is required.
