# Packet: Evidence log — rewarded acts (task 6.1a)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set.

List the **visible acts, honors, ranks, credentials, or prizes** that, in this window, the organization **rewards members for**. Use dated citations. This is an inventory, not a score.

Do not judge whether rewards were "earned." Do not ask whether rewards matched founding aims. Do not rate the organization.

## Output

A numbered list. Each item: name of the reward or credential; who grants it; what visible act it is for; date of a cited instance. If none found after search, write that.
