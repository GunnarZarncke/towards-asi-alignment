# Packet: Evidence log — result checks on listed items (task 6.1b)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case** bound (unit, window, sources) and a **numbered list of rewards/honors/credentials** prepared by someone else. The list has no extra commentary.

For **each numbered item**, quote or cite any procedure that checks attainment against a concrete result (an outcome other than the honor being awarded). If you find none after search, write **none found after search**.

Do not label items yes or no. Do not compute a fraction. Do not comment on the list as a whole. Do not rate the organization.

## Output

One row per input item: item number; citation or quoted check; or "none found after search."
