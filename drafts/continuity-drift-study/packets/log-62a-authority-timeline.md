# Packet: Evidence log — who held review or sanction authority (task 6.2a)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set.

Produce a **plain timeline** of who held **review or sanction authority** in that window: offices, bodies, and named holders where known, with appointment and end dates.

Do not evaluate whether review was fair. Do not list the content of verdicts (another worker may log those separately). Do not use the word "capture" or discuss conflicts of interest as a thesis.

## Output

Chronological rows: date or range; office/body; person if named; how appointed; citation. If none found after search, write that.
