# Packet: Evidence log — reversals and sanctions (task 6.2b)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set.

Produce a **plain log** of **reversals or sanctions issued, and against whom**, in that window.

Each row: date; who issued; against whom; what reversed or sanctioned; citation. Include acquittals or failed attempts only if the source records them as such.

Do not decide whether the issuer was "independent." Do not build a timeline of offices (another worker may). If none found after search, write that.

## Output

Dated event list as specified.
