# Packet: Evidence log — membership, wealth, reach, office (task 6.3a)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set.

Write an ordinary **institutional-history timeline** of **membership, wealth, territorial or market reach, and formal power** at dated points in the window.

This is a resource/size chronicle. Do not discuss whether the organization kept its founding aims. Do not mark "golden ages" as moral success. Do not overlay any other series.

## Output

Dated points: year; metric (membership count, revenue, houses, dioceses, market share, armed force, etc.); figure or qualitative size; citation. If series are missing, say none found after search for that metric.
