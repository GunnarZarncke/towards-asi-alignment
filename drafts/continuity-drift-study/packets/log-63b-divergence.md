# Packet: Evidence log — divergences from frozen aims (task 6.3b)

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, and **frozen founding aims** (fixed text; do not revise the aims).

Log **dated episodes** in which practice or official acts **diverged from those frozen aims**, with citations.

Each episode: date; what happened; which frozen aim it sits against; citation. "None found after search" is valid.

Do not rate how large the organization was at the time. Do not collect resource or membership figures. Do not explain "why drift happens" in general.

## Output

Dated episode list as specified.
