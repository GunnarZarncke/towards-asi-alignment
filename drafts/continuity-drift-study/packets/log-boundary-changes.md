# Packet: Evidence log — who obligations extend to

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, and the precisioner's description of who fell inside obligations at **window start**.

Log **dated changes** in whom obligations extend to (who is protected, bound, eligible, excommunicable, citizen, member).

Each change: date; from; to; instrument (statute, bull, bylaw, etc.); citation. If none found after search, write that.

Do not score expansion vs contraction as good or bad.

## Output

Dated change list as specified.
