# Packet: Evidence log — written rule followed, stated aim not served

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, frozen founding aims.

Log dated episodes where a **written rule or procedure was followed** and a **frozen founding aim was not served** (or was set back). Cite both the rule and the aim.

Do not score frequency as a rating. Do not discuss "spirit of the law" as a scale. If none found after search, write that.

## Output

Dated episodes: date; rule cited; aim cited; what occurred; sources.
