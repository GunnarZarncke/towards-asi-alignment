# Packet: Evidence log — detected divergence later reversed

## Cover

You are assisting a comparative study of continuity and drift in long-lived organizations.

## Assignment

You receive **one case**: bound unit, window, source set, frozen founding aims.

Log dated episodes in which a **divergence from frozen aims was detected and then reversed**, and name the **office, rule, or process** that did the reversing.

Each episode: date of divergence; date of reversal; mechanism; citation. If none found after search, write that.

Do not rate mechanisms. Do not list unsuccessful detections unless sources mark them as failed reversals.

## Output

Dated episode list as specified.
