Read REVIEWING_FOR_AGENTS.md for the thesis (grounded, human-correctable value-bearing processes across capability growth, ontology shift, successor creation, and multi-agent selection) and for live bridges MB1–MB11 including MB4a, with MB8 retired. (fiction, despite the prescreen), Alignment Midtraining Cracks Under Pressure, Training with conflicting values can induce CoT override, Social Sanction Gaming, and Homeopathic Learning. Additive score asks whether the post gives the book or field news a separation, stress test, or instrument that shipped cards do not already carry. 5 = a measured separation a chapter can cite. 4 = a result, named mechanism, or institutional instrument worth a cite or a card, with the overlap stated. 3 = a real hook that is an essay, a cluster sibling, a forecast, or a partial overlap. 2 = high-prescreen interest that restates a shipped card, a covered incident, or a capability note. 1 = duplicate of the named skip set (HF postmortem/talker-doer, Ward monitoring, Anthropic risk report, Astra safety case, I can see everything, embedded evaluators, Dwarkesh RSI/Christiano), or no analysis at all. Ward's monitoring survey was not itself in the high set; posts that only repeat it were scored 2 or 3 when they added an exploit or a taxonomy. Bridges and chapters are calibration against that gist, not a claim that Lean proves the post. Scores are judgment. Posts marked not fetched in full can move if the paper contradicts the LessWrong summary.
