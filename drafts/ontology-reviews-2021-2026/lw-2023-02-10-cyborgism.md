## Source ontology

Cyborgism (Dupuis / janus) changes the unit of intelligence from either “the human” or “the AI” to a tightly coupled **human–simulator loop**. Agency that coordinates cognition stays on the human side (preferences as conductor); GPT supplies capabilities as a non-agentic simulator—divergent, myopic, high-variance, many-hatted—via high-bandwidth tools (e.g. Loom) rather than as an outsourced assistant. This replaces the default automation frame (automate pipeline slots; fill GPT’s “missing pieces” of goal-directedness, coherence, grounding, robustness) and is defined against agents, genies, oracles, and RLHF’d research assistants.
