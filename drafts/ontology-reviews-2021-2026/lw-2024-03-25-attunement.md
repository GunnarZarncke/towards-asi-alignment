## Source ontology

Joe Carlsmith isolates **attunement** as a dimension of normative epistemology: meaning-laden receptivity to the world—living at the coal-face of what one cannot yet cleanly represent or control. It is a *mode* (yin, presence, Buber-style encounter), not a proposition. It replaces two reconstructions of “green”: knowledge-as-model-and-mastery (blue: science, prediction, Chesterton-fence humility) and terminal-goals-as-strongest-desire (red: Hume’s slavemaster). The thing received is often *the same facts with new authority*—realization rather than belief-update. The AGI stake is a future of Hume-bots that ask only “what do we want,” losing the life-thread of this mode; Carlsmith analogizes the wanted alternative to CEV as organic self-adjustment, not conditioners “figuring out the right values” and then executing.

Grounding assumes checked abstractions already exist; attunement is what you do when they do not. CCI preserves causal force of human correction, not how humans know what to correct toward (neither facts-then-values nor desire-king-of-the-hill). MB8’s retirement removes the CEV-process object Carlsmith uses as the future-shape analogue. Collapsing attunement into grounding or “see what matters” is a hostile-test rename.
