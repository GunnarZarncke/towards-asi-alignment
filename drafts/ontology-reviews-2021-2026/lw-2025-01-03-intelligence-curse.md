## Source ontology

The post names the **intelligence curse**: once AGI substitutes for human labor, powerful actors (states, firms) lose the return on investing in ordinary people—education, wages, welfare, political responsiveness—because revenue and regime survival no longer run through human productivity or citizen threats. It replaces the default “AI is a productivity tool that raises human potential” object with a **resource-curse analogue**: AGI as a rent-like factor of production, like oil, that severs state–citizen and employer–worker feedback. Intent alignment of AGI to those actors does not dissolve the curse; it can lock it in.
