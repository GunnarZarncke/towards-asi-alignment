## Source ontology

Abram Demski proposes **judgement** as one epistemic type covering what classical Bayesianism splits: agent-generated predictions (probabilities) and environment-generated evidence (observations). In the prediction-market / logical-induction picture, a resolving judge is just a wealthy trader; prices and trading strategies play both roles. What remains is a quantitative **collector–investor** continuum (Deductive Process as pure collector; Occam traders mixed; profit-seekers investor-like). It replaces the hard type-cut between prediction and evidence, and the special status of 0/1 Bayesian observations. The AI claim: truth-oriented systems need this dual nature on “squishy” questions where argument-strength is on par with final-answer feedback (a bridge to his “complete feedback” note).
