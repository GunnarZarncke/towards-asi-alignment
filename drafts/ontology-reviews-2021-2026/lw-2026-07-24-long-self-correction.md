## Source ontology

Wei Dai names **Long Self-Correction** as a replacement for AI Pause and Long Reflection. Pause has no until-when or for-what; Long Reflection implies the bottleneck is time to think (or AI-amplified thinking). The new unit is a **civilizational competence process**: whether humanity becomes epistemically, morally, and institutionally competent enough to safely wield transformative power. The load-bearing state variable is progress on interlocking human flaws that remain hard even with AI help—no workable moral framework, bad philosophy and long-horizon strategy, miscalibration about that incompetence, morality-as-status-game, unacknowledged positional values, manipulability, not knowing how philosophy works, and over-optimistic partial solutions. Hope is historical mysterious progress *if* the environment that permits that progress is preserved and no one is given power to permanently derail it.

## TSA coverage

- **Status:** partial
- **Closest TSA terms/chapters:** civilizational limit C-011 (ch45–ch48); \(U_H\); CCI in the large / civilizational self-modification (ch25); pause as non-discharging policy handle (field glossary); CEV as process not destination (ch45).
- **Overlap:** ch45 already asks whether humanity can author its own value change under amplification, not whether AI preserves current values; technical work preserves observability, reversibility, deliberation, non-manipulation, and correction channels, and cannot decide growth vs corruption. ch25 treats correction in the large as civilizational self-modification whose party-in-the-loop is humanity’s capacity for legitimate value formation. TSA already notes humans as confused, status-bound, and ignorant, and already refuses to treat pause as discharging MB1–MB10.
- **Gap:** TSA’s object is **preserving** \(U_H\)/CCI so legitimate update remains possible. Wei Dai’s object is whether operator competence **actually increases** enough to justify wielding power—explicitly not more reflection-time and not pause-until-safety. TSA has no unit that splits channel integrity from operator competence, no named rival to Long Reflection, and no bottleneck list of flaws that stay hard under AI assistance. Renaming C-011 “Long Self-Correction” would stretch a preservation claim into a self-improvement success criterion.

## Applicability to TSA

- **Score (0–5):** 3
- **Why:** Same neighborhood as C-011/ch25, but a unit shift TSA does not currently express. It should not replace CCI or the civilizational-limit cut: TSA’s technical role remains preserving the conditions of authorship, not waiting until humans are “ready.” It is worth a reverse-gap on the crosswalk so Pause, Long Reflection, CEV-as-destination, and C-011 are not collapsed into one handle. Wei Dai’s “rightly justify reshaping the universe” is a destination-shaped success test; ch45 already flags that pattern as the dangerous reading of extrapolation.
- **Ontology-stickiness risk:** High. Pre-2026 training is full of Pause and Long Reflection. TSA already *renames* that cluster toward a policy handle (pause) and a protected update process (\(U_H\)/CEV-as-process), so LLM-drafted TSA prose can treat C-011 as exhausting the ontology and miss the further cut (competence-to-wield as a process variable, not time-to-think and not channel-still-open). The 2026 primitive is short, recent, and easy to drop: TSA neither includes it, nor excludes it on purpose, nor sees the competence/preservation split.
- **Recommended action:** add-reverse-gap

## One-line finding

Wei Dai’s Long Self-Correction (human competence-to-wield as the process variable, not pause or more reflection) sits next to C-011/ch25 but is not channel preservation; reverse-crosswalk the unit gap.
