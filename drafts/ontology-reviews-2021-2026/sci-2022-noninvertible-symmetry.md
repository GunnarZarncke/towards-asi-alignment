## Source ontology

Gauging a finite 0-form (or higher-form / higher-group) symmetry of a \(d\)-dimensional QFT yields a dual symmetry generated by topological defects of every dimension up to codimension 1, not merely Wilson lines. Those defects need not invert: fusion can be a ring / higher-category rather than a group law. The paper’s primitive is a *universal* \((d-1)\)-category of dual symmetries, built from lower-dimensional TQFTs with the same symmetry \(S\) stacked and diagonally gauged; 2d condensation defects are the objects of a 2-categorical piece. What is replaced is the identification “symmetry generator = invertible group element / Noether charge.” Topological operators plus fusion (and higher morphisms for dimension-changing fusion) become the generators.
