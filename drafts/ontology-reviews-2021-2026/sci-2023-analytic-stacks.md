## Source ontology

Clausen–Scholze (IHES/MPIM course, Oct 2023–Feb 2024) rebuild analytic geometry so previously separate theories live in one categorical universe. **Light condensed sets** are sheaves on countable-weight (“light”) profinite sets: an honest Grothendieck topos that faithfully captures sequential / metrizable compactly generated spaces, cheaper than the 2019 full condensed category. An **analytic ring** is a (light) condensed ring plus a specified full subcategory of modules that count as complete. **Analytic stacks** are the geometries assembled from those. The intended replacement is the split toolkit of complex analytic spaces, locally analytic manifolds, rigid/adic spaces, and Berkovich spaces—archimedean and non-archimedean treated as instances, not rival foundations.
