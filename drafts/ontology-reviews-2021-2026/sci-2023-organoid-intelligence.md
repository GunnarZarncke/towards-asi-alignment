## Source ontology

Smirnova et al. recast a brain organoid from a *tissue model* of a brain into a candidate **trainable biological computer**: inputs, outputs, learning, memory, and closed-loop interfaces. The new unit is intelligence-in-a-dish — a living computational agent class, not an in vitro proxy for neuroscience and not (primarily) a moral-patient puzzle.
