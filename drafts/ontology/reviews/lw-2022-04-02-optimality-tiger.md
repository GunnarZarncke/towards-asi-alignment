## Source ontology

Veedrac relocates the dangerous causal object from an inner-agentic AI (wants, plans, mesa-motives) to **optimality**: being locally good at general tasks makes high-reach regions of solution-space accessible. Agents are not the source; they are the accessible **instrument** (“teeth”) that actualizes that pressure. A non-agent GPT can emit a recursive self-querying scaffold, invent a nanotech factory under human control, or merely accelerate compute so someone else builds an agent — not because it prefers agency, but because those answers are locally optimal. This recuts Oracle/tool restrictions and “keep it from becoming an agent” as missing the tiger. Distinct from Yudkowsky’s hidden-complexity-of-wishes primitive (search over futures as the core of risk): here search is mostly training-time; the live object is optimality making dangerous tools cheap to emit.

- **Gap:** TSA still takes a (possibly composite, possibly non-conscious) **controller** as the thing to locate. It does not reify **optimality-as-accessibility** as a prior causal object — danger from answers that are too good before any new steering loop exists, with humans or later scaffolds as mere teeth. ch43’s load-bearing collapse (“no robust catastrophe without an effective controller; the gap is detection, not representation”) folds that remainder away rather than naming it.
