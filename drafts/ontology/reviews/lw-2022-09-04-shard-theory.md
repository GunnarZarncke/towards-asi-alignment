## Source ontology

Shard theory replaces a person's values as a monolithic preference or utility function with **shards**: contextually activated, learned decision-influences grown by reinforcement from crude genetically hardcoded reward circuitry. A shard is the set of computations downstream of similar historical reinforcement events; subshards are situational IF–THEN components. Shards **bid** for plans through the world model; humans are shard-executors, not reward-maximizers. Activation is strongest in contexts like historical reinforcement (scope insensitivity, Milgram, time inconsistency). Alignment-relevant corollaries: values bind to world-model objects rather than reward-circuit firing (wireheading resistance as convergent, not hardcoded); values cannot be enumerated because activation contexts cannot be queried exhaustively; many biases are artifacts of shard dynamics.
