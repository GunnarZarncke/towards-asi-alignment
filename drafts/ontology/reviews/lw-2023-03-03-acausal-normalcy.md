## Source ontology

Critch replaces isolated bilateral “acausal trades” (nested-simulation bargaining with a named counterparty) with an **acausal society**: a population of civilizations that jointly set, via self-validating argument rather than nested simulation, what is valued and what is tolerated. The load-bearing unit is the **global normative equilibrium** (norms, externality conventions, embargoes), not a 1:1 trade or an “acausal economy” of producing what others want. A predicted content of that equilibrium is **boundary-respect**—consent-gated causal separation of living systems—because boundaries are cheap to specify and widely agreeable. The post denies that this equilibrium is physics: it does not automatically bind AIs or solve existential safety.

- **Status:** partial
- **Closest TSA terms/chapters:** inferential coupling / ICI; full acausal trade as pairwise limiting case (ch35); MB7d; composite-agent giant component; field map under acausal trade / ECL (appB, glossary). - **Overlap:** ch35 already treats residual non-message coordination as real, cites Critch 2020 for the FDT/trade limit, and makes pairwise/graph coupling an audit target rather than a stipulation. MB7d is the matching bridge. The source’s “do not over-focus on 1:1 basilisk trades” is compatible with TSA’s move from thought-experiment trade to measured residual correlation.
- **Gap:** TSA’s object is **whether** severed agents still co-vary (ICI, open inferential edges). Critch’s object is **what the society is coordinating on**: a shared normative fixed point with boundary conventions. TSA has no unit for that equilibrium, no split of acausal economy vs acausal society, and no use of “respecting boundaries” as a Schelling-point *content* of coordination. Renaming ICI “society” would stretch a detector into a moral-philosophy state variable.

## Applicability to TSA

- **Score (0–5):** 3
- **Why:** Same neighborhood as MB7d/ch35, but a unit shift TSA does not currently express. It should not replace ICI or change the measurement cut: Critch himself says acausal norms do not solve AI safety and are not automatically discovered by AI. It is worth a reverse-gap on the crosswalk so “acausal” in TSA is not only pairwise residual coupling / ECL trade. Boundary-respect as *predicted equilibrium content* is adjacent to TSA boundary-discovery only by homonym; merging them would be a hostile-test failure.
- **Ontology-stickiness risk:** High. Pre-2023 training is full of 1:1 trade, nested simulations, and basilisks. TSA already *renames* that cluster toward ICI, so LLM-drafted TSA prose can miss Critch’s further cut (society/norms as the relevant object) and treat measurement of coupling as exhausting the ontology. The 2023 primitive is easy to drop: TSA neither includes it, nor excludes it on purpose, nor sees it.
- **Recommended action:** add-reverse-gap

## One-line finding

Critch’s acausal society (global normative equilibrium, not 1:1 trades) sits next to ch35/MB7d but is not ICI; reverse-crosswalk the unit gap, do not absorb as boundary-discovery.
