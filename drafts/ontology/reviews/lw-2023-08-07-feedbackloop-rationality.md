## Source ontology

Raemon replaces rationality-as-a-repertoire-of-techniques with **nested error-correcting feedback loops** as the training primitive. The inner loop is a concrete graded exercise (his starter: Thinking Physics) plus metacognition that extracts transferable thinking tools; outer loops redesign the exercises and then the fuzzier, longer-timescale real-life loop. The distinctive claim is that **the loop is the primary product**, not the answers or the technique list. Hard domains (including x-risk research) are hard because they have the worst loops; the art is inventing shorter loops that still transfer. He contrasts this with CFAR/Leverage, which started from messy ultimate goals and stepped a little toward concreteness; he starts from the short/reliable end and works both directions along a concreteness spectrum, explicitly to avoid Goodharting proxies that do not track the real skill.

- **Gap:** TSA’s “feedback loop” is a system-dynamics object (composite agents, value compression, selection, civilization). It cannot currently name **a nested training unit whose product is a better loop for selecting cognitive strategies**, or the concreteness-spectrum design problem of inventing researcher-grade loops for first-try domains. Collapsing Raemon into CCI or Wiener restates the cybernetic slogan and loses the pedagogy/research-practice primitive.
