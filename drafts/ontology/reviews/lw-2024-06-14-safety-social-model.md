## Source ontology

Andrew Critch treats “safety” (and “alignment”) as a **relational** property of a technical idea × the human/social environment in which it lands, not as an intrinsic property of a technique. That replaces *per se* technical safety: obedience, interpretability, and truthfulness can all be misused or used to ship capabilities faster. The required state variable is an explicit **social model** of who receives the work and what they do with it; a safety-vs-capabilities spectrum and a safety-vs-shorter-timelines spectrum are both rejected as conflations (including conflationary alliances around those words).
