## Source ontology

Joe Carlsmith splits “thinking” from one activity into a correlated cluster of modes: map vs world, hollow vs solid, rote vs new, soldier vs scout, dry vs visceral. The primitive is whether cognition is actually coupled to territory, novelty, stakes, and truth, rather than generating thought-shaped representations (debate-form, party-line AI-risk talk, philosophy as map-play). He treats the cluster as the telos of a truth-engine and applies it both to individual inquiry and to civilizational “collective intelligence” as AI arrives. Scout-mindset, sincerity, concreteness, and skin-in-the-game are treated as nearby but insufficient.
