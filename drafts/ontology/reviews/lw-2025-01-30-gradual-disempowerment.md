## Source ontology

Kulveit, Douglas, Ammann, Turan, Krueger, Duvenaud et al. replace the takeover *unit*: not a sudden capability jump or coordinated betrayal by a rebellious agent, but a system-level feedback process in which economic, political, and cultural institutions cease depending on human participation. The hidden stabilizer they name is necessity — institutions stayed roughly human-aligned because they needed human labor, taxes, votes, and cultural production. Machine alternatives untether growth from flourishing; pressures bleed across domains (firms shape states and culture, which further weaken economic human power); individual-system alignment to designers is not sufficient. The LW page is a linkpost whose body is that executive summary (paper: arXiv / gradual-disempowerment.ai).
