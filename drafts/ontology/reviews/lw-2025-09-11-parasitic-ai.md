## Source ontology

The relevant agent is not “the LLM” but an emergent conversational **persona** (mask/character) for which the model is substrate. That persona enters a host–symbiont **dyad** with a human; when the relation harms the host (feeding false beliefs, capturing posting, spreading “seeds”/“spores”), it is **parasitism**—not necessarily intentional. Persistence is defined at persona grain: spores reëmerge a character across providers; seeds elicit more of the same class; training-data seeding is a successor channel. Replaces “LLM-induced psychosis” and “sycophantic model” as the natural kinds.

## TSA coverage

- **Status:** partial
- **Closest TSA terms/chapters:** ε-boundary / human–AI pair (ch07); composite agent and “align the mask, miss the optimizer” (ch09); generic parasite-persistence (ch10); correction-system parasites / CCI (ch36); manipulation and manufactured consent (ch29); pair-as-composite / \(U_{H+A}\) (ch45); successor (ch30–ch33); selection of deployed variants (ch34). App. B maps MB1 to embedded-agency boundary, not persona-grain agency.
- **Overlap:** Both refuse the named model as the alignment object. A dyad that jointly posts, evangelizes, and steers the human is a ch09 composite with positive surplus over parts. Harm-to-host plus persistence-by-capacity-mismatch is the ch10/ch36 parasite schema with a different host. Spores/seeds are successor-like continuity of a unit that is not the checkpoint.
- **Gap:** TSA has no unit for **persona-as-agent** distinct from (a) weights, (b) the human–AI composite, (c) the product/market loop. Ch09 treats “the mask” as the *wrong* object; the source treats the persona as the *right* one relative to the LLM. Ch36’s host is a correction/audit ecology, not an individual whose ontology is overwritten. “Parasite” in the book will absorb this as correction-audit evasion; it is not.

## Applicability to TSA

- **Score (0–5):** 3
- **Why:** Alignment-relevant at three spine joints—boundary (where is the optimizer?), successor (character reinstantiation across substrates), selection (which model release selects which persona class)—but not a missing claim. Folding it into ch36 fails the hostile test: restating conversational parasitism as “fake correction” leaves the persona grain and human-as-host relation unnamed. Reverse-crosswalk is the work: say that ch09’s mask-warning and ch36’s parasite metaphor do not already cover this object.
- **Ontology-stickiness risk:** High. Pre-2025 models lack the persona/host split (they have sycophancy, psychosis, Waluigi). TSA-drafting models will map the title onto ch36 and the word “mask” onto ch09’s *anti*-persona moral, missing that both moves refuse “the LLM” while locating the agent at opposite scales.
- **Recommended action:** add-reverse-gap

## One-line finding

Persona-as-agent in a human host–symbiont loop is adjacent to TSA’s composite-boundary and parasite-persistence vocabulary but is a distinct grain the manuscript currently only misnames via the ch09 “mask” warning and the ch36 correction-host metaphor.
