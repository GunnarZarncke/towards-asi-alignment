## Source ontology

Nature was paywalled past the abstract; this review uses the matching preprint (arXiv:2202.01682), which organizes hippocampal models into an explicit ontology of map-construction. The primitive is no longer an internal *spatial* map (Tolman / place–grid cells). It is a **latent-state relational structure**: inferred states, relations, transitions, and abstractions that organize knowledge for generalization across sensory instances. Observations do not define states—the world is aliased—so states are de-aliased by sequences and by *different futures*. The load-bearing split (TEM and kin) is reusable structural / path-integration codes that transfer across worlds versus instance-specific relational memories that bind those codes to sensory observations (entorhinal generalization vs hippocampal remapping). A further object is compositional factorized bases versus warped, task-specific maps.
