## Source ontology

Levin (2022) replaces binary “has a mind / is just physics” with an empirical **Self**: a scale-dependent owner of goals, compound memories, and credit assignment, larger than any part. Agency is graded on a **persuadability axis**—which intervention vocabulary is most efficient (hardware rewiring → setpoint rewrite → reward/training → rational argument)—not by origin (evolved vs designed) or substrate. Morphogenesis is recast as **anatomical homeostasis**: cell collectives solving problems in morphospace via stored target morphologies (often bioelectric), symmetric with problem-solving in metabolic, transcriptional, and 3D behavioral spaces. Selves nest and overlap (a holarchy of agents), can remodel during a lifetime, and are compared by **cognitive light cone** (spatio-temporal scope of setpoints / stress). Chimeric and synthetic architectures are first-class objects, not edge cases.
