## Source ontology

Sharma, Czégel, Lachmann, Kempes, Walker, and Cronin redefine the physical object: not a point particle or instantaneous configuration, but an entity specified by its *assembly space*—the recursive construction paths from elementary building blocks. The **assembly index** is the length of the shortest such path (computable; joins are physical operations, unlike Kolmogorov programs). **Copy number** is abundance. Together they yield an ensemble measure **assembly** \(A\): high index plus high copy number is taken as evidence of selection without first naming individuals or units of selection. History and causal contingency become intrinsic to the description of matter at chemical scale. Nested spaces: assembly universe / possible / contingent / observed. List status C (contested).

## One-line finding

Assembly theory makes construction history plus copy number the identity of physical objects; TSA’s nearby “memory lineage” and “selection environment” are different cuts, so the primitive is orthogonal and should not be absorbed.
