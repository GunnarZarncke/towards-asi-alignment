## Source ontology

Apruzzi, Bonetti, García Etxebarria, Hosseini, and Schäfer-Nameki recast a *d*-dimensional QFT’s generalized symmetries (higher-form, higher-group, non-invertible, categorical) and their ’t Hooft anomalies as one associated *(d+1)*-dimensional topological field theory—the SymTFT. That object replaces a bag of transformations (or even a list of topological defects) with a bulk TFT: boundary conditions select which global form is realized; bulk couplings encode anomalies; theories with the same local dynamics can differ in global symmetry structure, sometimes related by gauging. In their string constructions the local degrees of freedom sit at a singularity; the symmetry theory is obtained by reducing the topological sector of supergravity on the link/boundary of the extra geometry, including torsion via differential cohomology. Status A/B: ingredients (anomaly theories, higher-form symmetry) are older; the operational unit “entire symmetry/anomaly structure = bulk TFT, derivable without a Lagrangian” is the 2021–2023 recarving.

TSA’s load-bearing cuts (boundary, bundle, grounding, CCI, successor, selection) are operational process variables, not topological defect fusion.
