# CEV — coherent extrapolated volition

Yudkowsky 2004. Field default name for “our wish if we knew more, thought faster, were more the people we wished we were, and had grown up farther together.” TSA reads it as a pair with CBV and as a `ConstitutionalRule` instance (`cevConstitution`), not as a live route to correction integrity. MB8 is a gravestone.

## Formalization — 2

**Evidence.** The 2004 writeup names a process. It does not give a builder. Lean records `claimsExplicitBuilder = false` on `cevConstructionBet`. What TSA can check is its own wrapper: constituency, extrapolation, and aggregation all true (`specify-construct-instances.yml`). That schema is the book’s, not CEV’s math. `ConstructionCrux` for this target stays open.

**Example.** Two labs can both say they are “doing CEV” and there is no typed object either of them can fail. One means “run a deliberation people still own.” The other means “predict \(V^\star\) and optimize it.” Ch. 4 treats that collapse as the dangerous form, not as a theorem the original text already separated.

## Concreteness — 4

**Evidence.** Ch. 4, Ch. 28, and Ch. 45 call CEV one of the closest existing ideas because it already refuses a snapshot of present preferences. The manuscript’s own dangerous/safer cut is the concreteness: infer a final extrapolated value and optimize it, versus preserve the extrapolation process. Ch. 45 adds that humanity may not have one endpoint.

**Example.** A ruler who says “I know what the people would choose if they were wise; therefore their participation is unnecessary” is the failure mode Ch. 45 writes down. That is a real political object (present dissent classified as noise), not a toy ontology. The score is not 5 because CEV names that complexity and does not operationalize bearers, institutions, or successors.

## Crux interface — 4

CEV forces four questions and does not force four others. The score is 4 because the ones it forces are the outer-alignment core, and TSA had to type them. The 2004 text does not.

- **What sort of target.** “What we would want if we knew more” can be read as a deliberation people still own, or as a finished utility to optimize. Ch. 4, “Why ‘What We Would Want If Smarter’ Is Not Enough” (`sec:cev-not-enough`), and “The Object Sort of the Target” (`sec:object-sort-of-the-target`): a procedural constitution is one sort of object, not a second path to correction. Example: two labs both say “CEV” and one runs a council while the other predicts \(V^\star\) and overrides dissent.
- **Whose wish.** Extrapolation still has to say which people count. Ch. 4, “Plural Alignments” (`sec:plural-alignments`): a regime has to preserve a plural correction ecology, not one utility. Ch. 45, “The Political Problem” (`sec:political-problem-ch45`): some groups will call merger continuity and others will call it death. Example: “humanity’s volition” silently drops the dissenters the extrapolation classified as confused.
- **Present objection.** The dangerous reading treats disagreement as evidence that the objector is not yet extrapolated. Ch. 28, “Relation to Coherent Extrapolated Volition” (`sec:relation-cev-ch28`): compute \(V^\star\) and optimize it, then treat disagreement as insufficient extrapolation. Ch. 25, “Relation to Coherent Extrapolated Volition” (`sec:relation-cev-ch25`), makes the same cut against the correction channel. Example: “you would endorse this if you thought faster, so your refusal now is noise.”
- **Who gets deployed.** Even a legitimate extrapolation is selected by labs, markets, and states. Ch. 34, “The Selection Turn” (`sec:selection-turn-ch34`): alignment is selected or destroyed by the environment that funds and copies systems. Ch. 45, “The Political Problem,” is the CEV-specific version: no theorem makes the disagreement disappear. Example: a ministry adopts one extrapolated charter and procurement then copies only vendors who match it.

Not touched, which is why this is not a 5:

- **Where the optimizer is.** Ch. 7, “The Problem of the Hidden Boundary” (`sec:hidden-boundary`): the system that extrapolates may not be the system that acts. CEV assumes the extrapolator.
- **Who the values apply to after a substrate change.** Ch. 18, “The Bearer Problem” (`sec:bearer-problem`) and “Substrate-Change Continuity” (`sec:substrate-change-continuity`). “Whom” in CEV is which living humans count, not whether an upload or a copy is still the bearer.
- **Successors.** Ch. 27, “Correction under Successor Creation” (`sec:correction-successor-creation-ch27`): the child system can drop the extrapolation. CEV does not say how the child inherits it.
- **A hidden objective.** Ch. 10, “Strategic Opacity” (`sec:strategic-opacity-formal`): the training pointer can be the right target while the learned system pursues something else. CEV is a pointer, not that check.

## Terminology — 4

CEV is the name a mixed room already has. The words inside it are not stable. Own terms, then the standard term each one gets confused with:

| Their term | What they are pointing at | Standard term it is not |
|---|---|---|
| Coherent extrapolated volition | Idealized reflective wish of humanity, if extrapolation converges | Expected-utility coherence (one agent’s preferences avoid a Dutch book). Ch. 25, “Coherence, Expected Utility, and the Channel” (`sec:coherence-eu-channel-ch25`) |
| Volition | What we would want, not what we say we want | Stated preference, RLHF label, revealed preference |
| Extrapolation / “initial dynamic” | An ongoing reflective process Yudkowsky framed as a dynamic | A computed endpoint \(V^\star\). Ch. 4, `sec:cev-not-enough` |
| Grown up farther together | Interpersonal convergence under shared reflection | Population average, voting, CBV’s blend |
| Option-value when wishes spread | Don’t collapse disagreement into one wish | Quantilization, soft optimization, low impact (Ch. 27, `sec:low-impact-not-invariant-ch27`) |
| CEV as alignment target | A specify-side constitutional rule | Corrigibility, correction-channel integrity. Ch. 28, `sec:relation-cev-ch28` |

**Example.** “Coherent” in CEV means the extrapolated wishes converge. “Coherent” in an expected-utility argument means one agent is not Dutch-bookable. A speaker can use the short name CEV and still be heard as claiming a utility function, a voting rule, or a corrigibility property. Famous enough to score 4; not stable enough for 5.

## Funding / support — 2

**Evidence.** Checked September 2026. MIRI’s 2025 fundraiser plus early-2026 gifts left the org well funded (public posts: about $3.4M in the 2025 fundraiser, then $10M and $2.9M). MIRI’s own research page says the 2024 pivot moved the org to communications and technical governance, and that alignment research is a small part of the work. The agent-foundations team was dissolved. CEV remains the communal name. It is not a funded construction program.

**Example.** Counting MIRI’s runway as CEV support would score the pause/off-switch org as if it were still paying people to build an extrapolated-volition target. The score is for the target, not the parent brand. Leftover agent-foundations work at Eisenstat scale is not a CEV builder.
