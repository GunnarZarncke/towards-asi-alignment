# Iliad communal canon — lessons (rough)

Status: **concept sketch** (2026-09-02). Not a lane. Split from [`cousin-product-comparison.md`](cousin-product-comparison.md).

Iliad’s [Textbook from the Future](https://textbookfromthefuture.org/faq.html) is the closest *product-form* cousin: a communal living TOC, not a finished book. TSA should not become Iliad. It can steal a few coordination moves.

---

## What Iliad is selling (their words)

- The textbook from 100 years hence does not exist. The **questions** it must answer are guessable now; write that TOC down.
- The TOC is a **coordination instrument**: shared context for a research programme; onboarding for new researchers, experts from other fields, **and AIs**.
- **Selective synthesis** (“Big Tent but not Kumbaya”). Named theory lines (ARC heuristic arguments, Timaeus/SLT, debate, Simplex, reward learning, MIRI AF, Kosoy LTA, Wentworth NAH, AIXI, …). Favorites may not appear.
- **Living document**, Stacks Project analogy: wiki + linear textbook; rewrite the TOC when the important questions change.
- **Megaproject**, not a lone-genius plan. Contrasted with davidad: one person’s *plan for control* vs a communal *programme for alignment*.
- Lean/ATP (their Aug 2025 FAQ): not yet everyday research math; the hard step is formulating informal arguments.

**Shipped today (browse, incomplete):** landing + FAQ + [projects.html](https://textbookfromthefuture.org/projects.html) (many People: TBD), plus Intensives, fellowship, ILIAD conference, planned Alignment Journal. Score: Yes as the product they are building; Some as a readable textbook.

---

## Lessons (steal without merging)

1. **A fillable skeleton that is not the book.** Field hub ≈ TOC of questions; the manuscript is *one* filling, not the field’s canon.
2. **Reverse translation.** Other agendas write their crux in their own terms (parked Ngo column in [`field.md`](field.md)).
3. **AIs as a first-class reader.** `llms.txt` / reviewing-for-agents already exist; keep them current.
4. **Selective synthesis, stated.** Inclusion test already omits; say so instead of pretending completeness.
5. **Do not compete as the communal textbook.** Depth-first one-author(+AI) argument vs breadth-first communal TOC. Point at Iliad. The communal wiki that *exists* is LW (Arbital fold-in) — see [`lw-wiki-tags.md`](lw-wiki-tags.md).

Out of scope here: rewriting TSA authorship, standing up a Stacks-like wiki, merging with Iliad.
