# Resolution advisors

Off the manuscript and off the companion site. The published contracts do not appoint these names, a desk, or a panel. The host platform adjudicates.

Two uses:

1. **Other platforms that require an external judge.** Use the names and the protocols below when a venue will not list a question unless someone outside the platform is named to resolve it.
2. **Advice later.** The same people may be asked for advice when a resolution is unclear. Advice is not the verdict.

None of these appointments are confirmed. `ideal` in the old catalog meant "proposed," not "agreed."

## Desk protocol

For a platform that requires a named desk:

- The desk is the project author plus one independent reviewer, both named at listing.
- When the glossary and the paper's own freeze do not settle a load-bearing call, the desk publishes a short memo naming the evidence reviewed, the bar applied, and the YES, NO, or OTHER verdict.
- Readers have one week to object before the resolution is final.
- One expert acting alone does not resolve the market.
- If the two desk members disagree and the panel protocol does not apply, the market resolves OTHER.

## Panel protocol

For a platform that requires intersubjective ground truth. This applies only to Markets 3 and 17, and only to cases that lack mechanical ground truth.

- A pre-appointed panel of at least five independent judges.
- At least 80% agreement before the case is scored.
- Do not put a standing five-judge panel on every market.

On the published contracts, those cases are excluded instead: scored cases have mechanical ground truth, and they alone count toward the sample size and the rates.
