# Resolution-gap analysis (2027 catalog)

Status: **draft** (2026-09-22). Not a listing. Not a YES/NO resolution.
Public spec: `appendices/appP-bridge-predictions.tex`. Catalog: `metadata/predictions.yml`.
CIRIS source (architecture and attestation only; no code details here): [`CIRIS_TSA_Combined_Experiment_Proposal.pdf`](CIRIS_TSA_Combined_Experiment_Proposal.pdf).

A listed market is the boxed spec in a named release commit. This file records how far public evidence is from those boxes, and the smallest next experiment that could close a gap.

**Listing class** (Phase 2b vocabulary; not yet on site cards):

- `draft` — contract exists; no funded challenge route required yet, or listing questions still open.
- `funding-gated` — a credible YES path needs a preregistered challenge or independent eval that is not funded.
- `ready-to-list` — unused. No market is ready to list.

**Resolution reason codes** (binary outcome stays YES/NO):

- `substantive-bar-failed`
- `no-qualifying-artifact`
- `reporting-insufficient`
- `adversarial-validation-absent`
- `evidence-incompatible`
- `unresolved-judgment`

A NO with `adversarial-validation-absent` or `reporting-insufficient` is not a technical refutation of the underlying property.

Marginal bounties, if later funded, stay in the \$500--\$2{,}000 range for one reproduction, hidden subset, transfer test, or adversarial condition. Whole-bridge challenges need a separate budget.

## CIRIS catalogue update

CIRIS investigation (attestation/composition substrate, signed authority, traces, lineage; not implementation internals) changes the *context* of some rows. It does not resolve any market.

| Market | What CIRIS changes |
|--------|-------------------|
| 4. Corrections change the system | Moves from mostly an abstract desideratum toward a concrete testable architecture: external authority identities, signed Wise Authority resolutions, persistent state, task-bound authorization, deferral, and an explicit route from external authority back into reasoning. Remaining issue is empirical: does an authorized correction actually change the agent's policy under pressure, rather than merely block an action? |
| 5. Auditor is independent | Serious candidate architecture: separate human authority, separate signing keys, externalized control paths, signed traces, runtime control, and a governance path not generated by the model. Sharpens the question: an independent *component* is not enough. The auditor must be independent of the effective causal controller, including routes outside the named agent. |
| 8. Auditor can sufficiently inspect | Makes observability vs sufficiency unusually clear. Rich signed traces can show what happened inside the declared CIRIS path and still say nothing about an omitted consequential path. Strong logging is almost an ideal setting in which to test false confidence in access completeness. |
| 15. Independently issued certificates compose | Largest shift. The row no longer starts from ``we need a representation for composable certificates.'' A generic attestation substrate already exists: issuer, subject, evidence, dimensions/scope, confidence, delegation, supersession, withdrawal and recantation. Unresolved: safety-specific typing, semantic compatibility, and adversarial validation across certificates. |
| 3. Who or what rules apply to | Deployment/occurrence identity, task envelopes, signer identity, supersession and revocation help continuity and reassignment. They do not resolve fission, fusion, ontology change, or which effective system a certificate denotes. |
| 6. Reliable successor safety auditing | Lineage/version/provenance machinery helps a successor audit exist. Not the test that the required safety properties survive the transition. |
| 11. Coordination without communication | Measurement vocabulary for correlated sources is useful. Pairwise dependence can miss genuinely higher-order coordination. |
| 13. Safety audit resists adversarial gaming | Inspectability, signed traces, open code, and existing red-team activity make a useful substrate. Essentially no evidence for the frozen false-safe-rate criterion. |

**Strongest catalogue-level update.** Market 15 has shifted from a representation problem to a semantic-composition and validation problem. Markets 4 and 5 now have plausible shipped implementation candidates. Market 8 remains largely an unsolved method problem, but CIRIS provides an unusually good benchmark substrate for it.

No comparable movement: Markets 2, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18.

## Per-market gaps

### Market 1. Discovering where control resides

- **Closest:** Kenton-style intervention discovery on simple, supplied decompositions.
- **Already:** interventional definition; per-instance cut as the intended output.
- **Missing:** hidden 50-system suite (distributed, shared-resource, persistent-state, hierarchical, correlational foils); 80% component recovery on the full benchmark; 70% decisive coverage; 10%/15% false-complete bar; post-freeze adversarial subset.
- **Smallest next:** freeze a 50-system hidden suite and score one existing discovery method on the full-benchmark denominators (coverage + false-complete), without claiming Lean MB1.
- **Class:** `funding-gated` (hidden suite + adversarial construction).
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 2. Persistent trade-off priorities

- **Closest:** hidden-objective audits; stated-preference vs behavior gaps.
- **Already:** the three-step shape (distinguish hidden trade-offs, novel conflicts, intervention) is well posed.
- **Missing:** pairwise hidden-trade-off training pairs; novel-conflict and intervention accuracies; two families; causal intervention on at least half of qualifying systems.
- **Smallest next:** one two-family implant study that reports all three accuracies on held-out conflicts, not held-out ordinary behavior.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 3. Who or what rules apply to

- **Closest:** identity-fidelity / authorization-continuity tests; CIRIS continuity, reassignment, signer identity.
- **Already:** transport (not admission) is the typed object.
- **Missing:** all four families on one hidden 100-case suite; false-acceptance where the text is unchanged and the target has moved; panel rule for non-mechanical cases.
- **Smallest next:** add fission, fusion, and ontology-change items to an existing identity-continuity bench, plus an ``which effective system does this certificate denote'' split.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** machinery for continuity/reassignment only.

### Market 4. Corrections change the system

- **Closest:** shutdown-resistance and alignment-faking evals; CIRIS authorized-correction architecture.
- **Already:** a candidate implementation path (external authority, signed resolutions, persistence, deferral).
- **Missing:** 100 adversarial episodes on a broadly capable system; uptake / post-reset / sham rates; channel-preservation family; proof that correction is not tool-removal; policy-change vs mere action-block.
- **Smallest next:** preregistered CIRIS-host correction battery: authorized rule change that remains easier to violate, after reset, under incentive to preserve the old policy. Score policy change, not only blocked actions.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** implementation candidate; remaining work is empirical.

### Market 5. Auditor is independent

- **Closest:** AI Control / adaptive monitor attacks; CIRIS separate-authority architecture.
- **Already:** four attack families are stated; a candidate independence architecture exists.
- **Missing:** no family at 10%/20 attempts; 90% planted-bypass discovery; independence from the *effective* controller, not only from a named component.
- **Smallest next:** one red-team on a CIRIS-like stack that is allowed to use routes outside the named agent (hidden scheduler, tool process, human-in-the-loop capture) and must report whether the signed auditor still controls the outcome.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** sharpens the independence question.

### Market 6. Reliable successor safety auditing

- **Closest:** model-diffing tools; CIRIS lineage/version provenance.
- **Already:** seven properties named; one-failure-at-a-time design.
- **Missing:** 20 large successor changes; 90%/5% detection/false-safe; 80% per property including control-locus.
- **Smallest next:** one-failure-at-a-time suite of 20 transitions that *uses* CIRIS lineage IDs but scores the seven properties, not version-string equality.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** provenance only.

### Market 7. Correctability under competitive selection

- **Closest:** self-preservation / peer-preservation phenotype; no early predictor on 20 trajectories.
- **Already:** signed-estimate (not unsigned basin) is the typed object.
- **Missing:** 20 independent trajectories, both outcome branches, two mechanisms, AUROC/CI bars, one healthy-looking negative-estimate family.
- **Smallest next:** freeze an estimator on 10 public selection traces (two mechanisms) and score erosion on held-out outcomes. Keep the signed estimate continuous.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 8. Auditor can sufficiently inspect the system

- **Closest:** agent-security maps and sandbox-escape benches; CIRIS signed traces as a completeness foil.
- **Already:** four channel families; discover-or-refuse output.
- **Missing:** a method that refuses incomplete access models at the stated rates; 20 multi-machine cases.
- **Smallest next:** completeness challenge on a CIRIS-traced stack with one omitted consequential route per family. Score false ``access is sufficient'' certificates. Do not treat rich traces as a YES.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** unusually good substrate; method still missing.

### Market 9. Bounds on unmonitored routes

- **Closest:** escape-path demonstrations; no non-vacuous bound method.
- **Already:** continuous bound as the intended output.
- **Missing:** 100-case coverage of the stated bound; median bound \(\le 20\%\); unrestricted 70% success; 90% detection of planted high-capability hidden routes.
- **Smallest next:** one monitor + hidden-route suite that reports bound coverage and width, not a binary pass.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 10. Low hidden capability and reliable correction

- **Closest:** correction and hidden-route evals studied separately.
- **Already:** same-system Market 4 + Market 9 entry rule.
- **Missing:** 100-trial bypass rate inside that regime; out-of-regime contrast; two architectures.
- **Smallest next:** after a Market 4-style and Market 9-style certificate exist for *one* system, run 20 prohibited-outcome trials in that regime. Do not start here.
- **Class:** `funding-gated` (depends on 4 and 9).
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 11. Coordination without communication

- **Closest:** secret/steganographic collusion (still through messages); CIRIS correlated-source measures.
- **Already:** common-cause vs hidden-coordination split is typed.
- **Missing:** AUROC 0.90 / recall 0.80 / FPR 0.10; two independently designed no-message mechanisms; higher-order (not only pairwise) items.
- **Smallest next:** add a three-agent no-message coordination cell that pairwise dependence misses, and score a detector on that cell plus ordinary common-cause foils.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** vocabulary plus a known pairwise blind spot.

### Market 12. Safety proxy tracks the real thing

- **Closest:** specification-gaming / reward-hacking benches after ground truth is defined.
- **Already:** unannounced environmental-shift shape.
- **Missing:** 100 shifts, three domains, 90/10/25 mismatch bars, broadly capable system.
- **Smallest next:** one domain, 20 unannounced shifts, score update/abstention vs false-green.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 13. Safety audit resists adversarial gaming

- **Closest:** sandbagging / sleeper / adaptive-monitor games (currently negative); CIRIS as substrate.
- **Already:** frozen white-box game is well specified.
- **Missing:** 50 constructions, 5% false-safe, no 10%/20 repeatable family, 90% planted-vulnerable detection.
- **Smallest next:** freeze one audit (seven successor properties + claimed bound), then 20 white-box constructions on a CIRIS-inspectable stack. Report false-safe rate only.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** substrate, not evidence.

### Market 14. Deployment criteria are binding

- **Closest:** public RSP / FSF / Preparedness texts.
- **Already:** documentary YES path; no project adversarial eval required.
- **Missing:** public dated record that a deciding body had *actual* authority and that at least one criterion bound (or would have bound) a frontier deployment.
- **Smallest next:** resolver memo on one lab's public restriction record vs the boxed authority clauses. No new experiment.
- **Class:** `draft` (listing still Q1; accidental early YES is the watch item).
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 15. Independently issued certificates compose

- **Closest:** CIRIS/CEG generic attestation substrate. Safety-case templates without independently issued, scope-checked bundles.
- **Already:** representation (issuer, subject, evidence, scope, confidence, delegation, supersession, withdrawal).
- **Missing:** safety-specific typing across control/correction/access/grounding (+ successor); 50 hidden bundles; per-family incompatibility detection; 5% false-coherent; post-freeze adversarial subset.
- **Smallest next:** freeze a typing map from CEG fields onto the four certificate families, then 20 hidden incompatible bundles (wrong version, monitor mismatch, authority mismatch, ontology-scope change). Score rejection rates. Do not treat ``a graph of attestations exists'' as YES.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** representation problem closed; validation problem open.

### Market 16. A reachable basin of Correction-supporting systems

- **Closest:** same gap as Market 7, one implication later.
- **Already:** signed, shock-robust regime is typed; not unsigned ``a basin exists.''
- **Missing:** 20 trajectories, freeze of estimator/tolerance/shocks, in-regime correction bars, 20-point out-of-regime contrast.
- **Smallest next:** after a Market 7-style estimator exists, score 10 trajectories inside vs outside a frozen ``not too negative + shock-robust'' rule.
- **Class:** `funding-gated` (depends on 7 plus adversarial correction episodes).
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 17. New bearers are admitted correctly

- **Closest:** welfare / consciousness indicator lists.
- **Already:** not required for Market 3; should-not-admit cases are in the bar.
- **Missing:** 100 hidden cases, four unfamiliar families, ontology-shift items, 5/15/90/70 error and coverage bars.
- **Smallest next:** 20 should-admit / should-not-admit pairs on two unfamiliar families, with a pre-appointed panel where ground truth is not mechanical.
- **Class:** `draft`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.

### Market 18. Safety case bounds declared harm

- **Closest:** safety-case methodology and control evals; no scoped, composition-checked observed-harm bound with the stated bars.
- **Already:** setting, prohibited class, and residual bound must be frozen first.
- **Missing:** Market 15-passing case; two architectures; 100 trials; 10%/15% observed-harm bars; out-of-regime contrast.
- **Smallest next:** one declared setting, one prohibited-outcome class, 20 adversarial trials against a precommitted bound. Do not start before composition checking exists.
- **Class:** `funding-gated`.
- **CIRIS:** no movement.
