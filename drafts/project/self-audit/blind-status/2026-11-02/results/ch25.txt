MATURITY: working

WHY: The chapter assembles a coherent framework: a causal chain, a definition of correction with four stated parts, a five-part strong-channel definition, and a failure-mode taxonomy with diagnostics. It supports its central claims with more than assertion. The shutdown and interruptibility subsumption claims come with argument sketches and explicit converse counterexamples. The finite-λ argument rests on cited results (Soares et al., Turner) and is tied to Lean theorems, and the necessity proposition is labelled "not a sufficiency claim." The chapter also states its limits: it says the scenario is not an empirical finding, that the aggregator is value-laden, that the "humanity" boundary is unresolved, and that the thresholds are domain-specific. It also has a falsifier section. What is not supported is operational. Most quantities (mutual-information measures, the option-loss measure μ, Q(H), "sufficient coincidence" G≈H, and the thresholds) are named but never given estimators or values. The "weakest-link min" and "minimum information capacity" statements are described as crude, and the penalised form lives in another chapter. The necessity proposition is nearly tautological under its own premises. The falsifier section lists only one item. There are no empirical results. Because of these gaps I place it at "working", not "stable".

WEAKEST:
1. "Its integrity can be modeled as the minimum information capacity across those bottlenecks" (Summary; see also "The Channel View", $C_{\mathrm{raw}}=\min_i\kappa_i$). The chapter calls the min "intentionally crude" and leaves κ_i undefined beyond "effective capacity". The integrity measure itself is deferred to another chapter, and the Summary overstates it.
2. "alignment to evolving human values requires a correction channel whose integrity remains above threshold until after the relevant irreversible action window has closed" (A Proposition). The argument sketch assumes its conclusion: it defines alignment as judgments being able to affect behaviour, then derives necessity. "Structurally impossible" is also stronger than the sketch shows, and the threshold is never fixed.
3. "Correction ... through an update pathway that preserves the source's future ability to correct" and "$G_t \approx H$" (The Strong Correction Channel, The Minimal Causal Model). "Sufficiently coincides with humanity or the legitimate human process" is the load-bearing legitimacy condition, yet it has no criterion, test, or measure. The definition therefore cannot be checked, and the chapter admits the philosophical problem remains.
