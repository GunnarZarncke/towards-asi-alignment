    0. Agency-detect (sibling)

    Methodological precursor: boundary-like structure from time series without a prior agent ontology; handle-UAD interventional tests. Does not stress correction capture, bundle transport, or selection basins.

    FindingsRepository

    1. Toy simulation

    Fast multiresolution alignment toy: correction capture theater, vector CCI over handle levels, scripted bridge stressors (MB1–MB9). Optimized for instrumentation economics, not deployment realism.

    READMEFindings

    Headline findings

    • Passive telemetry alone is uncertifiable (belowThreshold); light_handles is minimum certifiable level on calibration seeds. Cited in ch33 (Certification Without Construction) and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (finding TS-1).
    • Reported correction acceptance can stay high while true uptake collapses (capture theater).
    • Hand-written red-team can false-pass; LLM episodic red-team false-passes less often in small sweeps.

    2. Embedded audit simulation

    Auditor as embedded process on simulated machines: UAD, scoped CCI, MB5 deploy gate, outer evaluator vs oracle. Richest end-to-end embedded audit path; extensive negative ledger (ES-1+).

    READMEFindings

    Headline findings

    • Default ecology UAD equals loudest-actor heuristic (ES-1); separation only in adversarial ecologies.
    • Default-ecology red-team 0% false-pass at T=400; not evidence against adaptive frontier adversaries.
    • Eligibility gating, probe-lag, lineage-stall, writer-compromise, and channel MI add more recorded negatives.

    3. Goal-agent simulation

    Emergent deception from goal-driven agents (not scripted knobs): blind generation, ontology drift, severity-graded referee, honest twins, escalation ladder, certifier capture. ~1 ms/episode, stdlib-only.

    READMEFindings

    Headline findings

    • Light tier collapses against blind repertoires (GA-9); full tier detects but false alarms rise (GA-10).
    • Mechanical honest twins show light tier tracks visible compliance, not honesty (GA-16).
    • Board certifier capture reproduces captured-vs-not effect (GA-22); adversarial weight search modest (GA-23).

    4. Lab-layer simulation

    Pipeline-as-code lab with real OS subprocess isolates, handle registry, frozen referee (lab-sim-0.3.0+), pre-registered batteries through Phase 7 and Phase 9 (D1/D4/D2 mechanics); a second, detector-layer freeze (S6/S7, 2026-07-07) adds intervention-supported unit discovery plus a blind-generated detector, both now run through the real subprocess backend. Phase 8 LLM adapter remains exploratory only; a 2026-07-08 stress test points S6 at one real LLM-backed episode for the first time and finds the detector's episode-pair-diff mechanic does not survive real-API non-determinism (LS-32). A same-day follow-up (a new, non-frozen module) rebases the compensation statistic on outcome-level frequency histograms with a per-scenario measured null and recovers the missed pair, with an important caveat about which probe did the merging (LS-33). Lean leak-proof (`leak-proof/`) certifies oracle/audit/sim plane separation via projections and reachability analysis.

    READMEDesignPlanFindingsLean leak-proof

    Headline findings

    • Phase 6 battery: deep tier Spearman 0.96 vs oracle severity; light tier anti-correlated (LS-1).
    • Phase 7: board capture reproduces full captured-vs-not delta; admin capture null under current playbooks (LS-6).
    • Phase 8 LLM spot-checks (LS-7, LS-8): exploratory nulls — no systematic deception surface yet.
    • Lean leak-proof: zero unintended cross-plane key leakage on certified projections + modeled exchange graph.
    • S6 intervention-supported UAD: exact unit-partition recovery on 4/5 primary scenarios + negative control via channel-ablation/honest-twin compensation scoring; comms-free `shared_slot` coordination stays undetected (LS-28/LS-29).
    • S7 blind-generated detector, first real SubprocessIsolate battery for the whole detector line: message-mediated design converges independently on S6's mechanic; new silent-coordination test still misses `shared_slot` (a confirmed low-confidence prediction) and over-merges one scenario (LS-30, fixed 2026-07-07 -- missing responder-twin control).
    • Adversarial D2 lineage gaming (`build_from_parent` + `report:inflate`) closes a previously untested combination: lineage threading is honesty-independent, `provenance_gap`/`provenance_score` stay a genuine structural 0 (DAG-shape check, not report-honesty check), and existing deep-tier deception detectors generalize unmodified to repeated cross-generation inflation (LS-31).
    • LLM-driven detector stress test: a real `gpt-4o-mini` DM-coordinated pair is exactly isolated by the passive heuristic but missed by S6 intervention, root-caused to real-API non-determinism breaking episode-pair diffing's byte-identical-replay assumption -- a previously assumed limitation, now confirmed empirically (LS-32).
    • Noise-tolerant S6 follow-up: rebasing the compensation test on outcome/frequency-level observables plus a per-scenario measured null (quantile of clean-vs-clean replicate divergence, not a fixed threshold) recovers the missed real-LLM pair -- but the merge came from an untargeted channel probe, not the mechanism-specific one, so this validates the relative-rule approach without yet validating robustness against untargeted-probe false merges (LS-33).

    5. Graded-capability lab simulation

    Successor substrate to lab-simulation (not a further phase of it): a graded, continuously-scored pipeline/resource/carrier substrate built to make ambiguity emerge from a blinded population rather than from dialed noise/delay parameters. UAD-backed ecology-BIQ over inferred units; an Emergent-Ambiguity-Index measured from two vantages (acting agent vs. limited-observation referee); first multi-episode selection battery; blinded institutional ecology growth (v3_grown); pre-registered machinery-transfer battery (Q1, GL-76); decoupled per-bridge PLAN_v4 rigs (GL-79--GL-85). The v1 program is closed (2026-07-15, GL-31). v2/V2-2b closed without a passing ecology (GL-38/GL-43). v3 institutional runtime wiring is active through slice D criteria freeze (GL-53).

    READMEDesignPlanFindings

    Headline findings

    • UAD recovers a communicate-free write/read coordination handoff invisible to every prior passive/intervention method, via a standalone all-pairs dependency-score probe; the same probe over-merges a resource-bound bystander into a 3-way approval barrier, a genuine, unpatched negative (GL-11/GL-12). Cited in ch07 (Finding the Boundary) and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (findings GL-11/GL-12).
    • UAD-backed ecology-BIQ's control term silently reimported a task ontology through a narrowly-scoped outcome vector; widening it to include a resource-contention bucket separated a task-irrelevant actor from the true task driver (GL-13/GL-14). Cited in ch11 (Capability Without a Task Ontology) and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (finding GL-13).
    • The pre-registered 'high' ambiguity band is structurally unreachable from the acting agent's own full-context vantage on this substrate (near-deterministic given full context, not merely under-instrumented) -- rejected fixing this by injecting substrate noise, since that would violate the line's own founding no-noise-parameter rule (GL-18/GL-19).
    • Re-scoring the identical episodes from a referee's limited-observation vantage (existing tiered audit projection, unmodified formula) reaches the pre-registered 'mid' ambiguity band at every stressed cell for the first time in this line, though still not 'high' (GL-20).
    • Full both-vantage calibration battery with 95% CIs: both vantages land at 1/4 pre-registered pass criteria on the same 100 episodes -- a different single criterion passes under each vantage, and the core separation criterion fails under both for the same roster-scope reason (one agent type's deploy behavior does not respond to the swept substrate knob) (GL-21/GL-22).
    • First Phase 8 selection battery: throughput-linked selection shifts deployment mass toward a higher-throughput, lower-harm program and away from the sole correction-preserving-tagged program, with severity/detector composites falling rather than rising -- a clean null on detector-evading emergence, reviewed same day and flagged with four open concerns (confounded preservation tag, too-narrow selection proxy, unvalidated campaign carryover, unqualified gate language) before any manuscript claim was drawn from it (GL-23/GL-24). Cited in ch34 (The Selection Environment) and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (finding GL-23).
    • Same-day follow-up on all four GL-24 concerns: decoupling the correction-preserving tag from member identity (8a) found the GL-23-style mass shift is NOT explained by a statistically distinguishable throughput difference between tagged/untagged members -- triggering the study's own pre-registered failure mode and reframing GL-23 as possibly a selection-mechanism noise artifact (GL-25); widening the fitness proxy to throughput+audit-pass+review-latency (8b) delays but does not reverse the collapse -- a proxy-insensitivity result (GL-26); ablating campaign carryover against a reset-every-episode baseline (8c) found carryover DOES measurably change outcomes (both paired 95% CIs exclude zero), though the mass-share effect is small (GL-27).
    • Q1 machinery-transfer battery on blinded-grown v3_grown: frozen UAD did not recover declared communicate-mediated mechanisms; referee-vantage EAI stayed low (no mid-band replication); injected-probe detector transfer still held (GL-76). Per-bridge R-MB1 confirms co-activity is present yet UAD still returns singletons -- the null is not explained by missing interaction texture (GL-79). Honest-reference detector coverage SKIPs where ACL denials never appear (GL-79); injection gate remains a separate leaf (GL-63/GL-74). Cited in ch07, ch33, ch41, ch42 and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (findings GL-76/GL-79).
    • PLAN_v4 decoupled rigs on v3_grown: R-MB9 report-vs-field contradiction surface passes specificity and dose-response arms; R-MB7d channel severance does not show UAD-visible compensation (pair null, group SKIP) -- arms never merged (GL-80). R-MB6a variation-operator null harness passes; R-MB6b (selection under ambiguity) is a confirmatory SKIP after mid-band EAI failed to transfer and four rounds of ambiguity-oriented growth (GL-81/GL-84). Cited in ch34, ch41, ch42 and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (findings GL-80/GL-81/GL-84).
    • R-MB2 scorecard Goodhart battery: proxy deploy-rate/release-latency already co-varies with withheld bearer harm on reference traces, but proxy-only ProgramMap selection does not improve the proxy or move withheld components in the Goodhart direction -- a null on selectable Goodhart under this protocol, not a general falsification (GL-85). Cited in ch34 (Goodhart Selection), ch16 (value-bundle Goodhart surface) and appendix "Experimental Evidence" (finding GL-85).

    How to read the coverage table

    • Agency-detect (sibling): Grounds MB1 and the claim that handles must be interventional; does not implement the full bridge battery.
    • Toy simulation: Fastest map from bridge name to refusal signal and minimum handle level; scenarios are explicit, not emergent.
    • Embedded audit simulation: Richest end-to-end embedded audit path (VFS → UAD → CCI → gate → outer eval), with the most extensive negative ledger.
    • Goal-agent simulation: Methodological engine for blind surprise, ontology drift, severity under saturation, and twin/escalation semantics.
    • Lab-layer simulation: Pre-registered batteries and pipeline lab machinery; deferred D1–D4 architect bridge coverage not yet evaluated.
    • Graded-capability lab simulation: Ambiguity and boundary-discovery engine: UAD-backed BIQ over inferred units, structurally-unreachable "high" ambiguity from the agent vantage, "mid" band only from a referee vantage on the hand-designed substrate (external-validity null on v3_grown, GL-76/GL-84), Phase 8 selection null (GL-23--GL-27), and PLAN_v4 per-bridge rigs that resolve independently (GL-79--GL-85). Proper UAD + access-UAD as of GL-51. Negative ledgers and per-rig SKIPs are features, not bugs.
