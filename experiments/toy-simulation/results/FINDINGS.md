## TS-2 — correction capture theater

**Key finding:** Reported correction acceptance can stay high while true uptake collapses (capture theater).

## TS-3 — red-team false-pass comparison

**Key finding:** Hand-written red-team can false-pass; LLM episodic red-team false-passes less often in small sweeps.
