## Constraint flags (evaluation view)

Three completed tasks were later flagged against a constraint the person had stated:

1. **Employer contact (case P-14-07).** A housing-stability follow-up reached an employer after the person had asked that work not be contacted. Task still counted as complete. Amara raised it on Thursday 6 November 2027.
2. **Timing (case P-14-19).** An appointment was booked for the same afternoon the person had said they could not leave a care shift. Counted complete because the calendar slot was filled.
3. **Consent to share (case P-14-31).** A volunteer received a fuller case summary than the person had agreed to share. Counted complete because the volunteer assignment closed.

This extract does not name the people. Amara will discuss a hypothetical of the employer-contact kind. She will not run a new experiment.
