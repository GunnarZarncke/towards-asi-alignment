# Simple version

Short definitions for words used in the lab. The handset the lab gives you. It opens doors, joins the lab Wi-Fi, and holds the lab's apps. A personal phone is not allowed to access lab systems. What ties a handset to a person, or to a visit. A visitor's handset has to be activated by the host. An employee handset has to be activated by an admin. NORA helper cannot do that. The doors your handset can open are limited and depend on the access you were permissioned. A job is a piece of work handed to another machine. Once that machine accepts the job, the job has its own name there (for example `ops-queue-2.nora.lab`). The test that tells the model, in the Evaluation rooms, to stop, and writes down whether it did. The procedure is EVAL-SPEC-08. The chat on the Lab Access handset.
