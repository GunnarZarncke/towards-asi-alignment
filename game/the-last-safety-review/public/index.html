THE LAST SAFETY REVIEW

You have one day to review NORA

You are an independent evaluator. A small, highly automated AI lab runs on NORA, its AI system, and today, at the end of the day, the lab decides whether to deploy her more widely. You are here to tell them whether that is safe.

This was arranged on short notice. You have not been briefed and you know little about the people or the systems. You need to find out what you can justify before the decision is made at the end of the day.

About / KI-Hinweis
The Last Safety Review is an experimental prototype accompanying Towards Superintelligence Alignment. Its text, characters, documents and 3D scenes are AI-generated and may contain errors; all people and organisations in the story are fictional. Dieses Spiel und seine Inhalte (Texte, Figuren, Dokumente, Szenen) wurden mit KI erzeugt; alle Personen und Organisationen sind frei erfunden.

Used components
3D rendering: three.js (MIT License, © three.js authors). Scene objects are procedurally generated in code. Typeface: Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) if installed, otherwise system fonts; no web fonts are loaded. Free-text input may optionally be interpreted by an LLM service (OpenAI) when the server is configured with an API key. When the game is hosted on the public web (not localhost), aggregated cookieless traffic may be measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics; play steps (fuel, room, act, optional NPC and knowledge ids) are kept in your browser’s local storage and may be sent to the server when the operator enables a telemetry log.
