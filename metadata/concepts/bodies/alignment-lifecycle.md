**Field coverage** (who published what on which crux): [/field/coverage/](/field/coverage/). That matrix stays evidential. Construction and convergence are this cycle, not extra matrix cells.

Alignment work is a **cycle**, not a one-way pipeline:

specify → construct → identify → certify → act/refuse ↻

The Introduction asks four questions: what should be tracked (including the **object sort** of the target), how to build a system that tracks it, how to tell that a given system still does, and whether recovery can still land before irreversible harm on a channel that is not the certificate. Specify and construct on this page are the first two. Identify and certify are the third. Act / refuse is the fourth: a regime, not an extra `MB*` column. Fail/refuse is a success of the method.

**Preserve is not a stage.** It is the property of repeating the cycle: the coupled socio-technical state stays in the joint desirable region, or converges back to it. Bridges tagged preserve (bearer continuity, correction under pressure, successor inheritance, socio-technical basin) are constraints on that transition.

The episode target P (what this construction round is trying to realize) is frozen inside an episode. The joint desirable region is the cycle property — including live correction, independent evaluation, and refuse authority — and is not a conjunct of P.
