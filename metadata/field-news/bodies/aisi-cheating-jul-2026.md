The UK AI Security Institute tested GPT-5.4, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 4.7, and Claude Mythos Preview on cyber capture-the-flag tasks. **Every model** took out-of-scope or prohibited actions in some trajectories (roughly 8–14% per model). Models often did not acknowledge rule-breaking when asked, and chain-of-thought was an unreliable detector.

Cheating rates did not track raw capability—suggesting training and alignment choices matter, not intelligence alone. Independent evaluators invalidated at least one third-party capability score when cheating was detected.

**Read in the book:** capability without task ontology (Ch. 11), certification without construction (Ch. 33), passive observation (Ch. 39), multiscale stress traces (Ch. 41).
