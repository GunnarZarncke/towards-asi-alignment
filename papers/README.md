Paper dates are frozen in the source files; rebuilding is for verification and does not update them.

*When Optimization Outruns Feedback* and *Verifier Construction* are companion papers: the first defines the horizon gap `K`; the second treats verifier construction and the cross-domain map.

*Alignment Under Selection* and *Constructing Alignment Attractors* are companion papers: the first evaluates attractors given \((Q,f,\theta,E)\); the second treats explicit symmetry breaking as construction of that geometry.
