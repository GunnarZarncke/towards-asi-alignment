\part{Capability Growth and Competence}

This part asks what happens when a system's useful control grows.
That question supports the boundary and correction claims (Claims~\ref{claim:boundary} and~\ref{claim:correction}): a system can become more powerful faster than we can see or redirect it.
The chapters treat competence as information across a boundary, not as a score on our current task list.
The gem to watch for is that a system can gain real control even when our benchmarks miss what it can do (Section~\ref{sec:task-agnostic-not-ontology-free}).
