\part{Human Values as Needs Smoothed over Time}

This part introduces the value-bundle model. It argues that human values are compressed, embodied, social control structures with learnable low-dimensional aspects, but that preservation also requires tradeoff geometry, bearer maps, and adversarial measurement, not merely familiar moral words.
The gem to watch for is the middle path: values are not arbitrary labels, but the learnable bundle geometry is only safe while tradeoffs and bearers remain connected to the right world and survive measurement pressure (Sections~\ref{sec:four-part-definition},~\ref{sec:bundle-bearer-map}, and~\ref{sec:from-geometry-to-measurement-ch19b}).
