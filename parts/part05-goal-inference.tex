\part{Interpreting a System's Goals}

This part upgrades goal inference into transport analysis. It asks when rewards, intentions, words, and apparent goal continuity actually preserve value-bundle geometry, bearer maps, correction processes, and successor constraints across changes in system and ontology.
The gem to watch for is the transport hierarchy: the words can survive while meaning, value geometry, bearers, correction, or successor inheritance silently fail (Section~\ref{sec:four-layers-transport}).
The existing-work subsumption is that reward learning and CIRL-style cooperative inference become local projections of a stronger target: preserve bundle structure, bearer maps, and the correction process that produces later evidence (Section~\ref{sec:scalar-reward-hides-correction}).
