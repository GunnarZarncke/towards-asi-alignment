\part{Successors, Reproduction, and Continuity}

This part asks whether alignment survives what a powerful system creates, delegates to, copies, or becomes. It turns successor creation into the central test, names the conserved properties that would have to persist, and separates certification of a restricted class from full construction understanding.
The gem to watch for is the successor test: if a safety property is not inherited by delegates, copies, and replacements, it was probably a training artifact rather than an alignment property (Section~\ref{sec:successor-alignment-condition-ch30}).
