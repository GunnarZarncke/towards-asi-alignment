\part{Attractor Basins and Socio-Technical Selection}

This part moves from systems to the environment that selects them. It asks whether labs, markets, states, users, benchmarks, and AI coalitions reward correction-preserving systems or select those properties away, and it introduces the alignment attractor as a way for safety-relevant artifacts to survive institutional pressure.
The gems to watch for are the preservation envelope and correction parasites: selection can reward systems that consume correction capacity, so artifacts must make envelope-satisfying systems more fit than plausible imitations (Sections~\ref{sec:minimal-model-selection-ch32} and~\ref{sec:operational-definition-ch34}).
