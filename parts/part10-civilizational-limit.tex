\part{The Philosophical and Civilizational Limit}

This part asks what technical alignment cannot decide by itself. It treats value change, unconscious drift, merger, upload, bearer persistence, and final synthesis as civilizational questions: technical systems can preserve the conditions for legitimate change, but cannot settle legitimacy alone.
The gem to watch for is the update envelope: alignment should not freeze present values or outsource moral growth, but preserve the grounded, plural, correctable conditions under which value change can remain ours (Section~\ref{sec:role-technical-alignment-ch41}).
