## Do not conflate

- Load **1** with load **3**: selector hygiene is **Meta** (how the problem is typed); acausal trade is a **coordination** claim after severance.
- Load **2** with load **1**: predictor capture is an **object-level** failure mode; Sleeping Beauty selectors are not the same object.
- **Anthropic (lab)** with any load above: capabilities company (RSP, interpretability) — use “the lab” or the org name in prose.
