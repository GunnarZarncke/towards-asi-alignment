## Introduction

BlueDot Impact provides introductory Technical AI Safety and Frontier AI Governance courses that serve as a standard on-ramp for people entering AI safety careers. Strong pedagogy transmits shared field vocabulary and problem framings rather than resolving technical cruxes. Graduates enter the [Field hub](/field/) with a common baseline for reading bridge-crosswalk material and concept cards such as [alignment-as-measurement](/cards/alignment-as-measurement/).

**Who carries it:** BlueDot Impact

**What they aim to do.** Provide accessible, high-quality on-ramp courses so newcomers can orient in AI safety and enter research or policy paths with a common baseline.

**The hard question.** Strong pedagogy and career placement do not imply a unified research agenda; courses mainly transmit vocabulary and problem framings rather than resolving technical cruxes.

**What they produce.** BlueDot runs introductory Technical AI Safety and Frontier AI Governance courses that serve as a standard on-ramp for people entering AI safety careers.

**Key terms.** Key terms include [Technical AI Safety](https://bluedot.org/), [Frontier AI Governance](https://bluedot.org/), introductory alignment curricula, and a course pipeline that transmits shared field vocabulary via the [Field hub](/field/) and [glossary](/glossary/).

**What they contribute.** A shared preparadigmatic vocabulary layer for new researchers—useful background for reading bridge-crosswalk material but not a technical discharge path.

**How this project treats it.** Training throughput and curriculum quality are independent of whether any particular alignment mechanism can be verified under adversarial pressure.
