## Problem

There are only fragments of solutions to superintelligence alignment. By taking multiple independent methods together, there is a workable overall approach that addresses or at least approaches most of the issues from Yudkowsky's List of Lethalities and other analyses such as by Critch and Soares, and Hubinger.

## Approach

The simplified core of the approach could be described as:

1. discover entities that pursue goals dynamically instead of looking only at designed AI agents.
2. align not to a utility function or behavior but to something that values flourishing of all kinds of agents
3. ensure that a correction channel remains that allows influence on the above.

Material for this approach is collated in the Towards Superintelligence Alignment repository — developed further into blog posts, interactive websites, and a book with companion site.

Mostly cover personal expenses for the duration of the grant. Some part of the money would go to compute costs for scaling up experiments already being done (mostly unsupervised agent discovery).

## Milestones

- Collate and integrate existing alignment research lines into one manuscript and companion site.
- Publish decision-relevant artifacts: concepts, bridges, experiments, and reading paths for funders and researchers.

## If it works

A legible, integrated alignment roadmap — book, Lean spine, experiments, and companion site — that funders and researchers can navigate without adopting the full technical ontology first. Unsupervised agent discovery plays a significant role in the alignment approach above.
