**Role.** Protocol. Graph node `control`.

> AI Control studies protocols around untrusted models under intentional-subversion threat models. Its monitor games and safety rates are protocol evidence. They do not supply a moral target.
>
> — Frames draft §4.9 (Greenblatt et al. 2024)

A protocol family for deployment when the objective is untrusted. It composes as a leaf of a safety case. It does not replace the correction relation.

Example: [Split Personality Training](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/og7km7vmJ6Ktay9Ds/split-personality-training-revealing-latent-knowledge-1) (Dietz et al., 2026) adds a non-influencing “honest persona” that reviews the main reply from shared activations—monitor-style evidence on misalignment, not a replacement for correction.
