**Role.** Target. Graph node `criteria_vector`.

> A vector does not choose its scalarization, Pareto solution, bargaining rule, or lexicographic order.
>
> — Frames draft §4.2 (Roijers et al. 2013)

Pareto optimality on task coordinates does not entail a correction floor. The red edge to the correction certificate is a non-entailment until weights are chosen (`viaProjection`). Do not call a stakeholder vector a CCI score.
