**Role.** Target. Graph node `human_power`.

> Across the two versions, the proposal can instrumentally favor confirmation, caution about irreversible changes, and forms of corrigibility. … Human power therefore does not automatically imply legitimate correction.
>
> — Frames draft §4.3 (Heitzig and Potham 2025/2026)

Heitzig and Potham give a parametrizable aggregate of modeled human goal-attainment. Adverse possibilities already recorded include belief manipulation and refusal of pause in some cases. On the correction-centered graph it composes as a positive direction inside an admissible region, not as TSA’s object.
