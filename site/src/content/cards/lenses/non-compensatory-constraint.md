**Role.** Constraint. Graph node `deontic`.

> These frames are not merely large rewards. A prohibition can remain binding no matter how much task performance is available elsewhere.
>
> — Frames draft §4.7

Constitutional text can be read as a hard constraint, a pairwise order, a lex priority, or a scalar. Only the first reading is this node. Exact-penalty theorems can restore equivalence for a particular program; they are not free (`deonticScalarIncompat`).
