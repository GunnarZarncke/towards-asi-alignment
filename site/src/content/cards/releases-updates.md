This companion site tracks **manuscript releases** as short cards so readers can see what changed without opening the PDF changelog.

**How versions work**

- **MAJOR** — structural milestones (numbering scheme, part/chapter architecture, or a declared manuscript milestone).
- **MINOR** — new or substantially rewritten chapters, appendices, or framework objects.
- **PATCH** — fixes, calibration, citations, and editorial passes.

Browse the [Updates page](/updates/) for the newest release first, or open a version card below. The canonical long-form changelog remains [`RELEASE_NOTES.md`](https://github.com/GunnarZarncke/towards-asi-alignment/blob/main/RELEASE_NOTES.md) in the repository.
