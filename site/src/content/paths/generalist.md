## What this path is for

You have not followed alignment Twitter, MIRI archives, or this project's prior papers. You still want a honest map of what superintelligence alignment *could* mean as a research program.

**You will leave knowing:**

- The five preservation problems the book treats as load-bearing (boundary, bundles, bearers, correction, successors, selection).
- Why "align the model weights" is often the **wrong object**.
- What would count as **evidence** here—and what the book explicitly does **not** claim.
- Where to go next if you want formal detail, eval artifacts, or policy translation.

**Time:** about 2–3 hours of concept cards; add Appendix D on the site if you want one end-to-end hospital-network gate.
